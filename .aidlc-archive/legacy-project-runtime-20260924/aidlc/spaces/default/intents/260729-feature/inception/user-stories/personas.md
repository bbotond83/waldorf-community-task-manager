# Personas

## Parent

Views linked-family duties, signs up, manages requests, and receives Hungarian reminders without exposure to other families' restricted data.

## Kindergarten Administrator

Operates records, assignments, approvals, privacy lifecycle, imports, and organization-wide reporting.

## Group Administrator

Coordinates group-scoped tasks, families, events, and permitted approvals.

## Read-only Staff

Views relevant schedules and coordination display names without contact or sensitive profile access.

## Super Administrator

Administers organization configuration, roles, imports, and audit access.
