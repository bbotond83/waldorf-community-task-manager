# Privacy Audit Functional Design Questions

## Q1 — Retention Hold

How should a legal or operational hold affect lifecycle deletion?

- A. Suspend deletion/anonymization only for held records, record the hold/reason in redacted audit evidence, and require authorized release. *(Recommended)*
- B. Suspend every family lifecycle indefinitely.
- C. Ignore holds.
- X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A — Suspend deletion/anonymization only for held records, record the hold/reason in redacted audit evidence, and require authorized release.
