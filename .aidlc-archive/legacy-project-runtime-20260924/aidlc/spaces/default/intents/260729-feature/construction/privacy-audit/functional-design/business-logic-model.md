# Business Logic Model

## Lifecycle Processing

Annual review identifies families with no enrolled child. At last-child departure, a 30-day grace period begins. On expiry, eligible contacts/optional fields/private notes delete; permitted operational history anonymizes; the redacted audit minimum remains for one year.

## Holds and Audit

An authorized hold suspends only held records until authorized release. Lifecycle, hold, deletion, and anonymization events create access-controlled redacted audit evidence; sensitive values never enter audit summaries.
