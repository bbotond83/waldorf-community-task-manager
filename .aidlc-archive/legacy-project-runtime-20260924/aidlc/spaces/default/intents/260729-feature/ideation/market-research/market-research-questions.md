## Q1. Existing Alternatives

Which tools or processes should the MVP be evaluated against locally?

A. Excel only — it is the current and primary alternative
B. Excel plus generic tools such as Google Forms, email, or chat groups
C. A named product or vendor (please specify)
D. Not yet known
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A. Excel only — it is the current and primary alternative.

## Q2. Initial Reach

What is the expected first-year deployment size?

A. One kindergarten group (up to 30 families)

C. Multiple kindergartens or a broader community network
D. Not yet known
X. Other (please specify)
