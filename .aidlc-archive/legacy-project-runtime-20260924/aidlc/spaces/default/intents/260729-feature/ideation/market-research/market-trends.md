# Market Trends

## Category Signals

Current group-coordination products consistently make online self-service signup, schedule visibility, reminders, and targeted communication baseline capabilities. SignUpGenius describes automated reminders, participant messaging, and edit/swap functions; VolunteerSignup supports online signup sheets and reminder emails; TeamSnap combines schedules, availability, assignments, and communication. [market-1] [market-2] [market-3]

For this initiative, these signals validate the approved [intent statement](../intent-capture/intent-statement.md): replacing manual Excel coordination with a mobile-friendly web experience is aligned with category expectations, while merely reproducing a spreadsheet is unlikely to create enough operating value.

## Privacy and Local-Market Implications

The product handles family and child-related context. The European Commission states that organisations processing children's personal data on a consent basis need explicit parent or guardian consent up to the applicable age threshold. This does not determine the product's legal basis, but it reinforces the need to treat contact visibility and child-related data as privacy-sensitive design constraints. [regulation]

The confirmed market position is not maximal functionality or lowest price. It is a trusted, privacy-bounded Hungarian coordination experience for kindergarten families, beginning with multiple groups and up to 30 families. [Q2] [Q4]

## Product Response

- Treat self-service signup, reminders, schedules, and communication as table stakes.
- Preserve the approved family-owned assignment and role-based contact-visibility rules as product differentiators.
- Keep the MVP bounded; native apps, SMS/WhatsApp, payments, automatic rotation, completion tracking, and live Excel synchronization remain excluded by the approved intent.
