# Authorization and Sensitive-Field Contract

The machine sources are [`authorization-matrix.json`](authorization-matrix.json) and [`sensitive-fields.json`](sensitive-fields.json). Their stable versions are referenced by every verification evidence record.

| Role                                | Permitted scope                                           | Main allow boundary                                                                                        | Required negative boundary                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Parent                              | linked family and own requests/assignments                | duties of the whole community, notifications, own contribution, working-group list                         | unrelated family and sensitive fields                                                                                |
| Kindergarten teacher                | whole community                                           | schedule owner/assignee/status and required display names, working-group list                              | every mutation, contact/profile/reason fields, family records                                                        |
| Group Administrator ("Koordinátor") | granted working groups, while the linked parent is active | events, tasks, assignments, approvals and archive of the granted groups; family names only; working groups | other groups' items, community-wide items, family records and contact details, every advanced organization operation |
| Super Administrator                 | organization including governance                         | all approved administration, including family records and working groups                                   | stale/revoked authorization version                                                                                  |

## School calendar and cleaning rota

| Screen (route)                                                 | Resource                       | Who may open it                                                                                            | Everyone else                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tanév (`/admin/tanev`), Beosztás tervezése (`/admin/beosztas`) | `school_calendar`, `rota_plan` | Super administrator only: read, create, update, delete and publish                                         | Denied, including for a group administrator, teacher and parent                                                            |
| Beosztás overview (`/szulo/beosztas`)                          | `rota`                         | Parent and group administrator (read); a group administrator is also a parent                              | Signed-out visitors are sent to sign-in and receive no data                                                                |
| Beosztás overview, teacher (`/teacher/beosztas`)               | `rota`                         | Teacher (read)                                                                                             | Denied                                                                                                                     |
| Duty card (`/szulo/beosztas/[id]`)                             | `rota_duty`                    | A parent of the family that holds the week, including a group administrator who is a parent of that family | Parent of another family, teacher, a group administrator with no holding family, and a made-up id get the same denied page |
| Duty card, super administrator (`/admin/beosztas/[id]`)        | `rota_duty`                    | Super administrator (read-only, any published card)                                                        | Denied for parent, group administrator and teacher, and for a made-up id                                                   |

The database decides again on every call. A draft is never returned to anyone but a super administrator, and the overview carries only a family's id and display name, never a contact detail or sharing choice. The duty card has no swap or cancellation control in this version. A group administrator has a menu link to the overview at `/szulo/beosztas`; it opens in the parent view, where the group administrator is a parent of a family (BR24.1, BR24.2). A group administrator who is not a parent gets the denied page there. The super administrator opens a card by its address; the planning page does not link to cards yet.

## Parent directory, own account and family records

| Screen (route)                                                                             | Resource               | Who may open it                                                                        | Everyone else                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Szülők (`/szulo/szulok`)                                                                   | `directory`            | Parent and group administrator (read)                                                  | Denied page; signed-out visitors are sent to sign-in and receive no data                     |
| Szülők, teacher (`/teacher/szulok`)                                                        | `directory`            | Teacher (read); sees name, phone and e-mail of every parent and no other profile field | Denied page                                                                                  |
| Szülők, super administrator (`/admin/szulok`)                                              | `directory`            | Super administrator (read), group administrator (read, as a parent)                    | Denied page                                                                                  |
| Fiókom (`/szulo/fiokom`) and its commands (details, sharing, password, new e-mail address) | `parent_account`       | The signed-in parent, for their own record only                                        | Teacher and super administrator get the denied state; no command accepts another parent's id |
| E-mail-cím megerősítése (`/email-megerosites/<token>`)                                     | none (single-use link) | Anyone holding the link; the database checks the link and spends it once               | A used, expired or malformed link shows "A hivatkozás már nem érvényes" and names nobody     |
| Family and parent records, invitations and parent removal (`/admin/nyilvantartasok/...`)   | `record`, `invitation` | Super administrator only                                                               | Denied, including for a group administrator, teacher and parent                              |

Field visibility is decided in the database: a parent who did not tick sharing is returned by full name only, and the children of a family are returned only when every parent of that family shared. The browser search filters data already on the page and sends no request. A removed parent's sign-in user is banned and its sessions ended by the server after the database change is saved; a failure of that follow-up is shown to the administrator and logged.

All denials use the same non-enumerating application contract: HTTP 403 where an HTTP response applies, `AUTHORIZATION_DENIED`, no state mutation, no resource-existence signal, and redacted identifier-only evidence. Grant removal, and archiving a parent or family, is effective on the next protected request and clears protected cached navigation/data.

Sensitive contact, locality, optional-profile, protected-reason, participant, and unrelated-family classes are absent from unauthorized projections—not merely hidden in the UI. The dictionary also defines log, audit, fixture, screenshot, report, and lifecycle treatment.

The four current selectable profiles match the runtime catalog. Converted legacy kindergarten administrators use organization-scoped Super administrator workflows, including governance; Parent/Teacher/Group admin direct advanced requests deny. The workbook import no longer exists: `/admin/import` answers "page not found" for every role, and past import audit events stay valid. Old teacher bookmarks are compatibility-only redirects and never create a session. Historical reports remain historical evidence.
