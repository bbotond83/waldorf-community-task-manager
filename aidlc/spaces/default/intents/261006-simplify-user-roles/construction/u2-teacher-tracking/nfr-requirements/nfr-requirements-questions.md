# Teacher Tracking Quality Context

## Confirmed Requirements

NFR1 privacy and data integrity, NFR2 existing full quality gates and NFR3 naming/accessibility apply. Contracts explicitly introduce no new service availability, throughput, deadline or retry daemon commitment. Existing local performance checks remain applicable.

## Ambiguity Analysis

No new target choice is required for this bounded local role change. Production traffic, availability, retention and real backend capacity are unmeasured and not claimed. Functional review source/outcome handoff gaps are explicit NFR Design/code-plan obligations; their resolution cannot change approved product behavior. No fabricated answers.
