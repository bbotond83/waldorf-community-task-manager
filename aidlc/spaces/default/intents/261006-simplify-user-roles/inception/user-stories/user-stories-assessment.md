# User Stories Assessment

## Decision

Execute. This is a user-facing permission change affecting four personas, teacher navigation/tracking and conversion of existing grants. Independently testable stories will connect the approved requirements to design and verification.

## Rationale and Factors

Brownfield application; four available roles; read-only teacher access within assigned groups; deliberate promotion of former kindergarten administrators; privacy, revocation and unknown-role denials across several permission representations. These are more than a label-only refactor.

Stories will describe users' outcomes and safe transitions, preserve stable FR/NFR IDs and include positive and denial scenarios. Priorities are Must Have as established by requirements; delivery sequencing and implementation Units remain downstream decisions.
