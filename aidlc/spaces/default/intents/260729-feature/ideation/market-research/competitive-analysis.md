# Competitive Analysis

## Context and Comparison Basis

This assessment uses the approved [intent statement](../intent-capture/intent-statement.md) as the product boundary. The local baseline is the current Excel workflow; the intended rollout is multiple groups with up to 30 families. [Q1] [Q2]

Adjacent products demonstrate a mature category for volunteer and group coordination: SignUpGenius offers online signup flows, automated reminders, group messaging, and swap/edit support; VolunteerSignup focuses on simple online signup sheets and reminder emails; TeamSnap combines schedules, availability, assignments, and communication for parent-facing groups. [market-1] [market-2] [market-3]

## Feature Comparison

| Capability | Excel baseline | SignUpGenius | VolunteerSignup | TeamSnap | Proposed MVP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shared task/event schedule | Manual and fragmented | Signup-centric | Signup-sheet-centric | Strong | Role-based community schedule |
| Self-service signup | Manual coordination | Strong | Strong | Available for team workflows | Required |
| Automated reminders | Manual | Email and text reminders | Reminder emails | Messaging and notifications | 7-day and 24-hour reminders |
| Assignment changes | Manual | Edit and swap support | Edit signup sheets | Availability and team workflow | Parent swaps and approval-bound cancellations |
| Family-aware ownership | Not structured | General participant model | General volunteer model | Team-member model | Family-owned assignments |
| Hungarian, privacy-bounded roles | Not structured | General-purpose | General-purpose | Sports-team-oriented | Core differentiator |
| One-time Excel migration | Existing source | Import-oriented member handling | Spreadsheet-like setup | Roster setup | Admin mapping preview |

## Competitive Implication

The MVP should not compete on a broad volunteer-management feature set. Generic tools already cover core signups, reminders, and messaging. Its defensible niche is a trusted Hungarian coordination experience that combines family-owned commitments with explicit role-based visibility for a small kindergarten community. [Q3] [Q4]
