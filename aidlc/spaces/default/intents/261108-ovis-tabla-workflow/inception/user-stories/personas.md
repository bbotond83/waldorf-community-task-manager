# Personas

Roles are drawn from `requirements.md` and today's roles in `business-overview.md`. No real people are described.

## Personas

### Parent (Szülő)

- **Role:** a member of a family in the community. The family holds duties; the parent signs in with their own account.
- **Goals:** know what their family must do and when; find someone to swap with; take a fair share without being chased; get to know the community.
- **Pain points:** the spreadsheet is hard to read on a phone; nothing reminds them; they must work out by hand who to ask.
- **Tech comfort:** mixed. **Frequency:** a few times a month, mostly on a phone.

### Working-group coordinator (Munkacsoport-koordinátor)

- **Role:** a parent elected by their working group, holding the working-group admin rights for that group only.
- **Goals:** plan the group's work, keep its to-do list tidy, run its events and recurring duties.
- **Pain points:** task lists go stale; signing people up and checking what is done is manual.
- **Tech comfort:** medium. **Frequency:** weekly during busy periods.

### Teacher (Óvónő)

- **Role:** a kindergarten teacher, a member of the Teachers' Conference.
- **Goals:** see who is on duty; see what the working groups are doing; reach parents.
- **Pain points:** has to ask around to find out who is responsible.
- **Tech comfort:** mixed. **Frequency:** weekly.

### Administrator (Adminisztrátor)

- **Role:** the super admin of the community.
- **Goals:** keep families up to date, plan the school year, step in when something goes wrong.
- **Pain points:** planning the year and chasing empty slots by hand takes hours.
- **Tech comfort:** medium to high. **Frequency:** weekly, more at the start of the year.

## Relationships and Priority

1. **Parent:** the largest group, and most value depends on them using the app.
2. **Administrator:** without yearly planning there is nothing for parents to see.
3. **Working-group coordinator:** a parent with extra rights in one group.
4. **Teacher:** mainly reads.
