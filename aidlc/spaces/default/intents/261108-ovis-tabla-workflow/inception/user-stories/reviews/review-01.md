### Summary

The stories are well sliced, trace to every requirement and use Given/When/Then with authorization and privacy outcomes. The settled answers (Q2 to Q8) are applied correctly. Three gaps would make engineering come back with questions before building: the administrator's reassignment of a published duty has no story, the swap has no decline, expiry or conflict rules, and removing parents has no privacy criteria (NFR11 is deferred). Fix those three and the stories are ready. The minor items can be fixed in passing.
