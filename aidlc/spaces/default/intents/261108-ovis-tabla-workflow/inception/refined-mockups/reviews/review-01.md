### Summary

The layouts and the shared behaviour rules are sound, and the bottom bar, rota overview and animal-care sign-up are well covered. The stage is held back by gaps in the privacy-sensitive directory states, the account-lifecycle screens, the duty cancellation and weekend-pick cases, and the event and admin forms that many Must stories depend on. Engineering would have to ask what those screens contain. As this is an advisory pass, the owner can weigh these findings and request changes at the gate.
