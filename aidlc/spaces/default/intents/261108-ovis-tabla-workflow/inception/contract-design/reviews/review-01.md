### Summary

The contracts are broadly sound: one database, one transaction, a consistent envelope, and failure behaviour stated for email, the daily run and the migration load. Four Major gaps remain: two consumers that cannot reach their provider (C6, and C9 in its stated direction), one dependency edge with no contract (U8 to U7), and an incomplete duty and place creation contract (C4). They should be fixed before Functional Design builds on these boundaries.
