### Summary

The design is implementable and covers every acceptance criterion; the stated deviation from duty rows is coherent with ADR-003 and its consequences are acknowledged. Two items deserve the owner's attention before approval: the missing catch-up rule for due and overdue notices, and the unplaced schedule row in the lock order; the rest are small contradictions to tidy.
