### Summary

The design fits a local-only reality: no deployment workflow, one forward-only migration, revoke-all-then-grant, the privacy allowlist and invented seed data all follow team and project rules. The per-command lock list is sound for commands and removal, and the notice-window and removed-flag decisions are consistent with the design files. The two points to weigh are who recalculates rota-week occurrences after a republish (and the leftover contradiction in reliability-design.md), and whether `notice_send` inside the daily check breaks the lock order.
