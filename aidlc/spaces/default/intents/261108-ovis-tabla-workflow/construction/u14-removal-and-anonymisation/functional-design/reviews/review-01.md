### Summary

The design is implementable. The steps, the order, the permission rules and the reads match the migrations. The two Major points are specification gaps to close before code: where the ended account ids come from, so that removed parents' sessions really end, and the task and assignment lock order of the new family removal body. The Minor points are contract and wording fixes, plus one privacy decision for the owner.
