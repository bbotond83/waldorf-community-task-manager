### Summary

The migrations implement BR100 to BR124 soundly: limits are counted under the event lock, withdrawal works on closed events, the removal pair keeps the rows and anonymises them, the notices carry no names, and reach is correct for each role. The main gap is evidence and wording, not design: the fixed test list has no stated reasons for its missing cells (the one Major), and several small copy, accessibility and document inconsistencies should be fixed before the owner review. Verdict READY, with the removal-versus-sign-up race worth a closer look.
