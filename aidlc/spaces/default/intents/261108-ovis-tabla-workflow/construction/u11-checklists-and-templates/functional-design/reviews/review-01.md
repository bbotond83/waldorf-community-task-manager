### Summary

The design is largely sound: its rules trace to every AC and consumed contract, locks and idempotency are specified, and the cross-unit calls into U10 resolve. Two gaps need a decision before build: the version and reorder concurrency model for an event's checklist, and how C15's "carry over a claim" is possible when `checklist_add_task` takes no claimant. The rest are consistency and scope points that are cheap to settle.
