### Summary

The design is thorough and consistent with the requirements, rules and tech-stack decisions: the transaction, lock, once-key, audit and test designs hold up. Two gaps should be fixed before Code Generation: the missing book facts function that notice registration needs, and the authorization table, which is stricter than BR131.1 and BR135.1.
