### Summary

The design covers every story and acceptance criterion and follows the C1 lock order, envelope and idempotency. The owner's answers are honoured, and races between two borrowers, two returns, and removal against borrow are closed by the book-row lock plus the version check. Two Major gaps should be settled before approval: the contradictory "until when" limits, and the missing read that lets U14 list held books. The rest are Minor clarifications, so the verdict is READY.
