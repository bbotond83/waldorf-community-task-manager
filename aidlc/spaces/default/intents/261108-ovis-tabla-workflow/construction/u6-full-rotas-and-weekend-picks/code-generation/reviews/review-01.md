### Summary

The Unit follows the approved plan closely. The database enforces BR60 to BR67 as named functions with the full convention (session check, idempotency, version, U3R00 refusals, revoke then grant, family-then-week-then-place lock order). DUTY_INELIGIBLE reasons go only to super admins and the family concerned, and only family display names appear. The fixed test list is covered for the pick and assign commands, and all eight new database test files are wired into the scripts. One real gap remains against the plan: a picking family that is no longer allowed on its week still owes a pick that nobody can fulfil or be told about (Major); the rest are disclosure-level minors, so the verdict is READY with that finding for the owner to weigh at the gate.
