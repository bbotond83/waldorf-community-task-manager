### Summary

The design is coherent and implementable: the claim, retry and daily-run mechanics are sound, and the service-only and no-stored-address boundaries match C8, C9, C16 and ADR-006 and ADR-007. Two points need the owner's attention before code: the title model cannot yet reproduce today's state-dependent notice titles, and the "Try again" stale-screen token is undefined.
