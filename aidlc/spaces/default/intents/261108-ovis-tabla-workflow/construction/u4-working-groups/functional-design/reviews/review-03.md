### Summary

The design is implementable. BR40 to BR44 each give a trigger, a check order and a refusal. The no-isCoordinator decision holds, because isCoordinator appears only in the unstored GroupMemberView and the grant is the single record. Privacy and authorization outcomes are explicit in BR42.2, BR41.1 and BR41.5. The remaining points are minor contract-recording and reachability gaps (C7, C16 and the C2 changes).
