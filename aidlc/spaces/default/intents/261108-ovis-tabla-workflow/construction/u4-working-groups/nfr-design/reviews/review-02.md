### Summary

Seven of the eight prior findings are resolved, and the restated lock order is much better. But the order omits the authorization-version row that every grant change writes, and with the lifecycle's account-deactivation loop that still allows a deadlock between a coordinator's leave, revoke or appoint and a family's lifecycle run. Fix the step's placement and lock list, and this is ready.
