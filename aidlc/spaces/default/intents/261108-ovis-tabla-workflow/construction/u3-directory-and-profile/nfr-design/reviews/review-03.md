### Summary

The password-check bypass is closed and the address-key and lock-order wording is much clearer. One Major remains: the account-status step takes the account row before the family row, while removal and email confirmation take the family row before the account row, so a deactivation racing either can deadlock. Fix the order, and carry the three refinements into `rules.md` and `traceability.json`, and this is ready.
