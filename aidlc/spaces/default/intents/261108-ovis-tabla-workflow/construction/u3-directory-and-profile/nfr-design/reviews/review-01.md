### Summary

The design covers every NFR id and follows the functional rules, including the deliberate BR36.3 refinement, which sits in a different place from the rule it changes. The main risk is concurrency: the email-confirm lock order can deadlock with removal, and the "address taken" check is not serialised everywhere it is written. Both are fixable by stating the lock sequence, and the password-change audit gap should be recorded openly as an accepted deviation.
