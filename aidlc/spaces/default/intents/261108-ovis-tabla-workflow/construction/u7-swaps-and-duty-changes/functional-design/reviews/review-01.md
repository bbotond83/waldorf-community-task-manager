### Summary

The scope, states and ownership of this design hold up against the contracts. But the cancellation-approval path conflicts with what `place_release` actually does, and its follow-up step has no place to act on. The stored-version checks would refuse every acceptance or reversal as written, and an open cancellation on a started duty has no way to end. These need fixing before a developer can build it without guessing.
