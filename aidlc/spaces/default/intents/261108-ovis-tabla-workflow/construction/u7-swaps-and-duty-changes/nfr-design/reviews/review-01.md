### Summary

The security design is largely sound and consistent with the platform idempotency table. Two points should be settled before approval: the existing `change_requests` read policy can bypass the stated reason-visibility control, and the denied-audit claim does not hold for the current swap functions. With two Major findings, the review stays within the READY limit, but both would be cheaper to settle now than in Code Generation.
