# User Stories Assessment

## Decision

**Execute.** [requirements]

## Rationale

The corrective intent is user-facing across five inherited product roles and adds a developer/product-owner setup and acceptance journey. It must coordinate local runtime, role authorization, privacy-safe data, multiple application surfaces, browser evidence, and future real-target boundaries. Requirements alone do not provide independently estimable vertical delivery slices. [requirements] [business]

## Factors Considered

- Brownfield domain modules exist, but no application delivery layer exists. [components]
- The prior product has five roles and 14 normative stories. [prior-stories] [prior-personas]
- Local-runtime deltas span setup, identity, shell, product surfaces, quality evidence, and documentation. [requirements]
- Team practices require Given/When/Then testability, coverage, build/start, browser, accessibility, and security gates. [practices]

## Key Value Areas

- Reproducible fresh-checkout local execution.
- Privacy-safe role/profile switching and authorization evidence.
- Parent, staff, and scoped administrator acceptance surfaces.
- Synthetic-versus-real integration evidence separation.
- Quality-gated pre-deployment handoff.

## Mob Integration Outcome

Design, development, and quality participants agreed that the original Parent and Administrator items were acceptance epics. The normative mob addendum further decomposes remaining record/coordination breadth, gives FR-LR-14 an owned approval story, assigns inherited invitation/event-note/shared-validation gaps, and makes evidence accumulate alongside each product slice.
