### Summary

Four test schemas are enough for the phased design, the migration number and the NFR coverage are correct, and nothing contradicts the Q&A answers. The audit purge as designed cannot work: the existing append-only trigger refuses every delete on `audit_events`, which also breaks the reset and the clock-controlled test. The design also leaves open the per-schema account scoping in the shared Auth service, the real housekeeping function to change, and the stage order in `run-local-verification.ts`.
