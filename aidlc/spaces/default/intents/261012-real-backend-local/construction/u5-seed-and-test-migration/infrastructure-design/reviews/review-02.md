> R-01 Reviewer note: The specification now states the mechanism. The migration replaces `audit_events_refuse_change()` so an update is always refused and a delete is allowed only inside `audit_purge()` for rows older than 365 days. `db:reset` uses an owner TRUNCATE, which does not fire row triggers (the trigger in `20261012_platform_audit.sql` is `before update or delete ... for each row`). `scripts/db/common.ts` and `worker.ts` connect through `DATABASE_URL`, the owner `postgres` connection, which is feasible. A transaction-local `set_config(..., true)` is a sound guard because every non-owner role has its table privileges revoked. One wording slip remains and is listed under new findings.

> R-02 Reviewer note: `identity_housekeeping()` is named. Its latest body is at `20261043_u4_repairs.sql:212`, and its revoke and grant lines are at lines 495-509. The design re-creates it in `20261044` with the purge count in the result. The three tests that change are listed.

> R-03 Reviewer note: The design adds a `--schema` selector, per-schema addresses (`<schema>.<tag>@example.test`), ids derived from the schema name, and a reset that removes only accounts with its own prefix.

> R-04 Reviewer note: The stage list matches the key order in `run-local-verification.ts` (lines 31-52). `database-seed` is inserted after `database-notifications-import`. Unit and component tests and coverage are stated to be stack-free.

> R-05 Reviewer note: The job limit is raised to 90 minutes unconditionally, and the three-times retry multiplier is noted.

> R-06 Reviewer note: The SBOM job's placeholders, the evidence fields `runtimeMode` and `fixtureVersion`, and the runtime config are covered. The Validate job's equivalent gap is a new finding.

> R-07 Reviewer note: The Playwright project-to-schema map, the `beforeAll` reset helper, the retry behaviour and `workers: 1` are explicit.

> R-08 Reviewer note: The narrowed readings of NFR1.68, NFR2.35 and NFR3.20 are recorded in the specification and in `traceability.json`.
