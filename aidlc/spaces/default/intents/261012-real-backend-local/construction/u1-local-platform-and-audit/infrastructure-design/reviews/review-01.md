### Summary

The scope is right: no deployment workflow, a local stack and a CI database job. But the three security- and implementation-critical mechanisms (worker-schema exposure and reload, loopback enforcement, key model) are asserted rather than specified, and the CI integration does not reconcile with the existing `verify:local` workflow or NFR2.6. Four Major findings make this NOT-READY.
