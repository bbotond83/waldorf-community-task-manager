### Summary

The audit, idempotency, hold and runtime-config work is sound and well tested, and the 20261006 edit does not weaken the guard or the policies. Two Major gaps remain: the database tooling does not insist on a local target, and the privacy and secret scan exemptions (`source-materials/`, `aidlc/`, large and untracked files) could let real personal data through. Both are small fixes and should be made before the unit is accepted; neither blocks the verdict.
