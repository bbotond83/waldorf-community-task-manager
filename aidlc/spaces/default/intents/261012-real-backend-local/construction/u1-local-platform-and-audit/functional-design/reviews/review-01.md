### Summary

The coverage map is complete and the rules follow the confirmed answers, but the central designs are not yet implementable. The worker-schema exposure and default-schema drift (R-11 b, c) are still open. The audit function contradicts itself on denial and rollback, takes the actor as a caller-supplied value, and has no action list. The idempotency lifecycle also conflicts with its unique key. Five Major findings block readiness.
