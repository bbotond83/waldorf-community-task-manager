### Summary

The identity design is mostly sound: the active-session check, the pre-session function list, the password-verification hook with its real-stack tests, the compensation logic and the privilege lockdown hold up under reading. It has three Major defects: an invitation can end up permanently stuck in `accepting`, a signed-in person can change their sign-in email directly and break invitations and resets, and the unit-test instructions point at files that do not exist. Fix those and the unit is ready.
