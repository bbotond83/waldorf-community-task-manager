### Summary

The design is mostly consistent with U1's local-only stack and with CI, with no deployment workflow. Three gaps would force a developer to guess: how the service-role key reaches the app against U1's tooling-only key handling, the NFR1.7 https check that Q3 quietly drops, and a configuration read-back that the sign-in service's settings endpoint cannot perform.
