### Summary

The ledger, the staged-data privacy policy (BR41.7, BR41.8) and the audit use are sound, but the design cannot be built as written. The import screen needs several more changes than the one upload control, and the reminder job, notification mapping, workbook structure, commit state rules and system callers are inconsistent or undefined.
