### Summary

The cut is sound and covers FR1 to FR12, but the entity references hide two cycles (Identity with Access and Directory, Coordination with Notifications), and the central decision of enforcing contact and scope visibility in the database is under-specified (column-level hiding, denied states, database identity, transaction boundaries). Resolve those before engineering handoff; the first super admin and per-worker account isolation are further open design gaps.
